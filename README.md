🧠 How the AI Works
Minimax Algorithm

The AI evaluates all possible future game states and chooses the move that maximizes its chances of winning.

Alpha-Beta Pruning

Alpha-Beta Pruning reduces the number of nodes explored during search, significantly improving performance without affecting the quality of decisions.

Outcome

On Impossible Mode, the AI will:

Never lose
Always block winning moves
Always choose the optimal strategy
